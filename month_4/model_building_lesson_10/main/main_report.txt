BaseEstimator + TransformerMixin
These are two base classes from sklearn.base that you inherit to build a custom transformer. 
BaseEstimator automatically handles get_params() and set_params(), which makes your transformer 
compatible with tools like GridSearchCV. TransformerMixin gives you fit_transform() for free by 
combining fit() and transform() into one call. Together, they make your custom class behave like 
any native sklearn transformer.

SimpleImputer
A built-in transformer from sklearn.impute that fills in missing (NaN) values in your data. 
You choose a strategy — median for numeric columns (robust to outliers), most_frequent for 
categorical ones, or constant to fill with a fixed value. It learns the fill value from training 
data during fit() and applies it during transform(), so test data never leaks into training.

Pipeline
From sklearn.pipeline, it chains multiple processing steps into a single object. Each step's output 
becomes the next step's input. When you call pipeline.fit(), it runs fit_transform() through every 
step except the last (which only calls fit()). When you call pipeline.predict(), it runs transform() 
through all steps then predict() on the final estimator. This prevents data leakage and keeps your code clean.

ColumnTransformer
From sklearn.compose, it applies different transformers to different column groups in parallel, 
then concatenates the results horizontally. For example, numeric columns get scaled while categorical 
columns get encoded — all in one step. Each entry is a (name, transformer, columns) tuple. Unassigned 
columns are either dropped (remainder='drop') or passed through unchanged (remainder='passthrough').

RandomForestClassifier
An ensemble model from sklearn.ensemble that trains many decision trees on random subsets 
of the data and features, then combines their predictions by majority vote. 
It handles nonlinear relationships well, is resistant to overfitting, and provides feature importances. 
In the pipeline, it sits as the final step and receives the fully preprocessed feature matrix.
